Kilo Labs LLC

From the 808 to the World. For the warrior in all of us.

Kilo Labs LLC is a Honolulu company founded in 2025. We build combat sports gear — BJJ gis, rash guards, judo uniforms, wrestling gear for Hawai'i schools and clubs — sourced wholesale in the United States and finished with island pride. We study the masters-age athlete (50+) with science-based, field-tested methods. And we sponsor and spotlight Hawai'i youth athletes across the pipeline: Pop Warner to high school to college to the pros, girls' flag football, judo, and wrestling. Combat sports is the foundation. The horizon is a Polynesian performance brand standing alongside the greats.

Filed business nature (DCCA, Oct 23, 2026): Combat sports equipment and apparel sales and e-commerce; artificial intelligence research and experimentation support services for defense, space, and government applications.
